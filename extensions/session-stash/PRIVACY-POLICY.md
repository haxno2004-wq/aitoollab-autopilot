# Session Stash — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

Session Stash stores and processes **all of its data on your own device**.

- **What we store:** your saved tab sessions (tab titles, URLs, pinned state) in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). This data never leaves your computer and is deleted permanently when you delete a session, uninstall the extension, or clear extension data.
- **What we don't do:** no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no remote code, no data sales, no third-party sharing.
- **Network access:** the extension makes no network requests of its own. The single exception is an **optional** license-key verification against `api.gumroad.com`, which happens only if you paste a license key to activate Pro.
- **Favicons:** site icons are rendered from your browser's local cache via the browser's built-in favicon API — no requests to us or to third parties.
- **Payments:** if you buy Pro, the purchase and license key are handled by Gumroad under [Gumroad's privacy policy](https://gumroad.com/privacy). We receive only your license key and the email attached to the purchase, stored locally in your browser.
- **Contact:** questions → the support email listed on the extension's store page.

Changes to this policy will be posted with an updated date above.
